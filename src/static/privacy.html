Privacy

What we store, and why.

Company Brain is published by Refactron. It reads the tools a company connects, keeps what it reads in that company's own graph, and uses it only to answer questions from that company's people. Effective 18 October 2026.

Who this covers

This notice covers the Company Brain website, its console, its Slack app, and the Jira and HubSpot apps published as Refactron. The public page at this site's home reads a synthetic demo company only.

What we read

  • Slack sign-in. Your Slack user ID, your workspace's ID, your name and your email address, so we know who you are and which company you belong to.
  • The Slack app, when a workspace adds it: messages that mention it or are sent to it, and messages in the channels it is added to, so it can answer and remember what was decided. What comes from a private channel is only ever shown to members of that channel.
  • Jira, read-only, for the projects an admin picks: issues with their key, summary, description, status, components, reporter and assignee, and the names and email addresses of those people. We read the email addresses to link each Jira account to the same person in Slack.
  • HubSpot, read-only: companies, deals and their owners (names and email addresses), and which company each deal belongs to.

We never write to Jira or HubSpot, and we ask each for the narrowest read access that does the job.

Where it is kept

Each company gets its own graph database on servers we run in Google Cloud. The access tokens Jira, HubSpot and Slack give us are encrypted before they are stored. Sign-in sessions are kept for at most seven days.

How it is used

  • Only to answer questions from people in the same company, filtered to what each person may read.
  • Never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train models.
  • When a question is answered with the help of a language model, the question and the parts of the graph needed to answer it are sent to our model provider, Anthropic, under its commercial API terms.

How long it is kept

What a tool brings in is kept while it stays connected. What Company Brain remembers from Slack conversations is kept until it is deleted: email {{contact}} to have memories, a person or a whole company deleted, and say which workspace you are in.

Removing it

An admin can disconnect, in the console, any tool that was connected in the console. Disconnecting deletes everything that connection brought into the graph, its tokens and its settings. Removing the Slack app from a workspace stops it reading that workspace; what it already remembers stays until you ask us to delete it.

Contact

For questions about this notice, or to ask for your data to be deleted, email {{contact}}. Requests are handled privately, never in a public tracker.
