Set up Company Brain

Three steps: name your company, add the Slack app to your workspace, and connect the first tool it should read. You can come back to any of them later.

  1. 1

    Name your company

    Company Brain is in beta, so a new company needs an invite code. Your Slack workspace joins it, and you become its owner.

    From the email that invited you to the beta.

  2. 2

    Add Company Brain to Slack

    Added to your Slack workspace.

    The Slack app answers in your channels and DMs, and learns who is in your workspace, so answers follow who may read what.
